Each section is one release of WikiMusic: the version and the day it was cut in
the heading, and what a reader of the site will notice under /Added/, /Changed/
and /Fixed/.  A release is this section, an annotated =vX.Y.Z= tag on the commit
it describes, and the same words published on the forge.

The version follows SemVer.  A new thing is MINOR, a fix is PATCH, and a change
that takes something away -- an export, a flag, the shape of a file -- is MAJOR.
The first section is the next release: what has landed since the last tag waits
there under the headings below, unnamed until the day it is cut.

The file starts at =v1.19.0=; the releases before it are on the forge, at
[[https://github.com/jjba23/wikimusic/releases][github.com/jjba23/wikimusic/releases]].

:CUSTOM_ID: v1-20-2

* v1.20.1 -- 2026-11-21
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: v1-20-1
:END:

** Added
- The Scheme layer has an API reference: =maak docs= runs dokgen over the
  modules rather than over their text, writes a card per exported procedure
  with its signature and its docstring, and renders it to
  =doc/api-dist/index.html=.  The generated =lib/wikimusic-api= tree stays out
  of it on purpose -- its documentation is the specification =maak api-check=
  guards.
- A new section of the manual says how a request is answered -- the Artanis
  route, =serve-api=, the generated dispatcher and the JSON answer -- with a
  diagram of that path, and =(wikimusic api)= carries the same figure in its
  commentary, so the reference and the manual tell one story.

** Changed
- =channels.scm= pins Guix master =5cea8de= and the jointhefreeworld collection
  at =139880f=, which is where =guile-dokgen= comes from.  =maak diagrams=
  re-renders every =resources/diagrams/*.mmd= to the SVG beside it.

** Fixed
- The docstrings the reference renders are Texinfo: the =@@type= the seo builder
  documents is escaped, the =@itemx= lists =guitarpro= describes sit one item to
  a line, and =format-fretboard= is no longer exported, since the procedure is
  no longer there to export.

* v1.20.0 -- 2026-11-21
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: v1-20-0
:END:

** Added
- Editing a song is a page of foldable sections -- the song, its artists, its
  genres, its artwork and its links -- with the song itself open first.  An
  artist or a genre is linked by choosing its name from a menu, and the ones
  already linked are links to what they name, each with the button that removes
  the link.
- A version of a tab says which song it belongs to and links back to it, and the
  field it is written in keeps the columns it was typed in.
- Saving a version says so on the song it returns to, as saving a song already
  did.

** Changed
- The controls that drive a tab are part of the tab: the heading, the button and
  the two menus above a tablature are one strip on the sheet's own top edge, in
  the paper's ink, held at the top of the screen while the tab travels under it.
  The speed is one menu of lines a second instead of a step and an interval.
- The chords a tab names are marked as the page is drawn instead of when a
  control is pressed.  The button that marked them is gone, and the wash under
  a chord is lighter.

** Fixed
- A name holding an apostrophe -- an album called =Guns N' Roses= -- no longer
  ends the statement that saves it.  Every row a route creates is written
  through kracht's bound values, which double a quote, rather than through the
  model API Artanis generates, which spliced the value between two single
  quotes; a test keeps a controller from going back to it.

* v1.19.1 -- 2026-11-20
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: v1-19-1
:END:

** Fixed
- The tab's chord highlight decides by line: only a line whose every word is a
  chord name is marked, so a title's =Am= and a heading's =A= stay what they are.
  (=8bcc943=)
- A drag of the page's scrollbar stops the tab's auto-scroll.  The loop records
  each position it scrolls to, and a page found anywhere else is the reader's
  and wins.  (=c6ac0ae=)

* v1.19.0 -- 2026-11-20
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: v1-19-0
:END:

** Added
- Auto-scrolling travels at one steady speed instead of jumping a block of lines
  on a timer.  The two menus are one speed -- lines per step against seconds per
  step -- and are read while it runs, so changing either is felt under the hand.
  It ends on the last line of the tab, and a wheel turn, a touch drag, a scroll
  key or a second press stops it.
- A highlighted chord is drawn in the tab's own ink: a wash and a hairline under
  the letters, never a second colour, so it reads the same on white paper, on
  dark paper and on the page's own surface.  The box adds nothing to the
  characters, so the columns a tab is read in do not move.

** Fixed
- The bar keeps every one of its controls on a phone: one row of thumb-sized
  targets that scroll sideways, and below 640px the glyphs give way to words so
  each control still names itself.  The theme switch gained its word in all four
  catalogs (=theme=, =thema=, =tema=, =テーマ=).
- Images that had been drawn empty or oversized are drawn at their panel's size:
  the tile-size control's glyph, the donation figure, the practice block, the
  cover column and the detail column.
- The songs under an artist or a genre are readable: three to a row at 230px
  with the name whole inside the detail column, and two to a row on a phone,
  where the six-to-a-row size a list page uses left each cover 110px wide and
  every name cut short.
